Sword Art Online

Players are trapped in a virtual reality blockbuster where the game creator has disabled the log-out option. Survival is the only goal, as dying in the game means dying in the real world. To escape, they must conquer all 100 floors of a massive floating castle.

Description

"Death Note" is a cult psychological thriller about a battle of wits between brilliant high school student Light Yagami and the genius detective L. Light finds a mystical notebook dropped by the Shinigami (god of death) Ryuk, which kills anyone whose name is written in it. Light decides to rid the world of criminals to become a god of justice named Kira, but the police launch a massive manhunt to stop him.
